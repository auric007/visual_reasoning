## Analyzing the 40 examples to decide the next step what we can improve in VLM

### How are we checking?
For each original example **A**, we choose a donor **B** that the model correctly classifies as a **different class from its prediction for A**.
We run these five conditions:

|Check|Close image|Context image|What it tests|
|---|---|---|---|
|**1. Original**|A|A|Establish the original description and answer|
|**2. Replace both**|B|B|Does the response follow a consistent replacement image pair?|
|**3. Replace close only**|**B**|A|How does changing the candidate’s close view influence the response?|
|**4. Replace context only**|A|**B**|How do different surroundings influence the response, despite the unchanged close view?|
|**5. Gray both**|Gray|Gray|What does the model produce when scene content and target markings are removed?|

The **question, model, and decoding settings stay unchanged**.
