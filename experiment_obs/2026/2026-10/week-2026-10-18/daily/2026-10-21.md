# Current training Paradigm

## Input Order
Image 1: close view of the cyan-marked candidate
Image 2: wider context showing the same marked candidate

Question:
“The first image is a close crop of one marked candidate;
the second shows the same marked candidate in its wider
surroundings. The cyan box marks the candidate in both.
Choose its class from radar, missile, other, or background.
Think step by step, then answer.”

Both images enter together. We do not separately ask the model to analyze one image and then the other.
