## Raw Stage1 low-confidence audit for three radar IDs

Task: `RN-20261026-vlm-stage2-overlap`.

The researcher questioned whether three records marked `stage1_missed` truly had no Stage1 box at any confidence. The saved class-agnostic Stage1 files retain predictions down to confidence 0.001, while the VLM Level-2 pipeline admitted only boxes at confidence 0.50 or higher. Direct polygon-IoU checks against the permanent GT IDs found:

| GT ID | Best raw Stage1 confidence | Best IoU | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `18r01` | 0.1765 | 0.5460 | Valid matching raw box existed, but confidence was below 0.50 and it never reached the VLM. |
| `08r02` | 0.0626 nearest candidate | 0.0000 | No overlapping raw Stage1 box even at the 0.001 saved floor; nearest box was 7.61 px away at its closest edge. |
| `1Br01` | 0.2472 | 0.6838 | Valid matching raw box existed, but confidence was below 0.50 and it never reached the VLM. |

Therefore `stage1_missed` in the existing evaluation means **no qualifying Stage1 proposal at the configured confidence >= 0.50**, not always a literal absence from the raw low-confidence tail. For these IDs, `08r02` is a true raw proposal miss; `18r01` and `1Br01` are threshold-filtered misses. No VLM inference was run on the two recovered low-confidence boxes.
