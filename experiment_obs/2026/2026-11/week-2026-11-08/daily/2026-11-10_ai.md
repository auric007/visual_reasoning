### Current model and completed evidence

- The current verifier is Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct with the class-only four-way C1 LoRA.
  It receives a close view and a wider context view of the same candidate and emits
  one of `radar`, `missile`, `other` or `background`. Training used 1,764 unique rows,
  balanced to 1,968 rows per epoch for two epochs; text and vision-to-language merger
  LoRA were trained while the vision blocks stayed frozen.
- The balanced 256-proposal diagnostic reached macro-F1 0.553. The natural-prevalence
  clean Stage1 evaluation completed 4,231 proposals over 67 scenes: macro-F1 0.410,
  radar P/R/F1 0.196/0.568/0.292 and missile 0.249/0.585/0.349. End-to-end recall was
  0.355 for radar and 0.485 for missile.
- The inclusive all-80 audit completed 6,983 proposals, but includes 12 training-exposed
  scenes and one invalid-source case, so it remains diagnostic and separate from clean67.
- The aligned clean67 C1-versus-m0001 overlap is complete: 138 targets both correct,
  30 VLM-only, 66 YOLO-only and 153 neither. The oracle union is not a deployable fusion
  score because it does not account for false positives, duplicates or routing conflicts.
- The two recovered low-confidence Stage1 radar boxes (`18r01`, `1Br01`) were both
  classified correctly by C1 once forwarded, but two selected cases do not justify a
  global confidence reduction.
- The 40-example grounding audit was intentionally stopped at 200 interventions and
  analyzed offline. Its focused early-prefix follow-up completed 72/72 continuations.
  These show image sensitivity and text anchoring, not globally faithful explanations.
