## Where we are

The current best VLM is **C1 class-only four-way**:

- Base: Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct
- Inputs: close candidate image + wider context image
- Output: `radar`, `missile`, `other`, or `background`
- Training: 1,764 unique examples, two epochs
- LoRA: language layers + vision-language merger
- Vision encoder blocks: frozen
- No CoT required for C1
