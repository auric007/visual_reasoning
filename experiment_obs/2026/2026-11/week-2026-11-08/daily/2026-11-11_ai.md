### P0: paired radar transition audit on the same 119 Stage1 boxes

Compare the historical B1/A7 CoT model and the current four-way class-only C1
model instance by instance on the already-scored 119 Stage1-detected radar objects.
The aggregate changed from 58/119 to 64/119, but that net gain of six does not show
which objects changed.

Report four paired groups:

1. B1 wrong -> C1 correct;
2. both wrong;
3. B1 correct -> C1 wrong;
4. both correct.

For every wrong C1 case, retain its predicted class (`missile`, `other` or
`background`), scene, permanent instance ID and saved close/context images. Join
the available analyst radar attributes and summarize deployment state, antenna
visibility/type, platform shape, quality and scene concentration. Generated B1
rationales are model outputs, not visual ground truth; analyst fields and pixels
must remain separate evidence sources.

Primary questions:

- Did C1 recover the stowed/flat radars B1 called missile?
- Are the persistent errors still concentrated in the three weak radar scenes?
- Did four-way rejection create new radar -> other/background errors?
- Which analyst attributes distinguish recovered, persistent and regressed cases?

This is the best immediate experiment because it uses frozen saved predictions and
requires no GPU. It explains the small +5.0-point conditional-recall gain before
spending GPU time on a larger candidate sweep.

### P1 after P0: fixed-C1 candidate-gate benchmark

Then evaluate fixed C1 on lower-confidence Stage1 and/or current Stage2/Test116
candidates with all false positives, duplicates, candidate density, latency and
end-to-end precision/recall/F1 included. The two previously recovered low-confidence
radars are only selected examples and cannot establish a safe lower gate.

Success for the gate experiment means improved radar/missile end-to-end recall
without an unacceptable precision or candidate-cost collapse. Do not retrain C1
until this hand-off benchmark identifies whether candidate generation or verifier
classification/rejection is the binding limitation.
