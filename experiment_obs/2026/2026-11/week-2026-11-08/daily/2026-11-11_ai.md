### What is actually established about CoT

CoT is not intrinsically unable to learn the two target classes. On the positive-only
GT-box task, reviewed-positive R0 CoT reached macro-F1 0.727, and historical B1/A7
reached 0.690 on its combined radar/missile evaluation. The observed collapse is
the four-way rejection problem on natural detector proposals.

On the identical 256-proposal diagnostic, the R1 four-way CoT reference versus C1
class-only result was:

| Model | Macro-F1 | Radar F1 | Missile F1 | Other F1 | Background F1 | Non-target acceptance |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| R1 full CoT | 0.202 | 0.379 | 0.429 | 0.000 | 0.000 | 100.0% |
| C1 class-only | 0.553 | 0.602 | 0.662 | 0.450 | 0.500 | 25.8% |

This comparison used identical evaluation pixels, question, hardware and greedy
decoding, but the training data and output format both changed. It supports C1 as
the better current model; it does not prove that shortening the target alone caused
the gain.

Observed CoT failure patterns:

- **Generic rejection templates:** all 490 R1 `other` examples shared one full
  target explanation, and all 490 `background` examples shared another. They were
  not instance-specific analyst descriptions.
- **Template completion on absent evidence:** with two uniform gray images, all 40
  audited outputs were identical. Planning through Discrimination exactly copied
  the background template, while Synthesis and answer copied the other template.
- **Autoregressive anchoring:** after the model's Visual-evidence prefix was fixed,
  an opposite-class image pair changed only 4/12 answers; after Grounding was fixed,
  only 1/12 changed. Earlier generated claims strongly constrain later sections and
  the class token.
- **Wrong visual invariant:** 44% of missile training traces used absent
  antenna/mast/turntable evidence against radar, although stowed radars are often
  box-like and only 35% of radar traces mention an antenna. This teaches a shortcut
  rather than a robust boundary.
- **Negative-data mismatch:** R1 `other` was 62.7% vehicle and 21.0% smallvehicle,
  with no tank or literal arty; random backgrounds were not mined from the natural
  detector false-positive stream and were biased toward early sorted scenes.
- **Visible label shortcut:** R1 rejection images rendered class-bearing IDs such
  as `other_####` and `background_####`. This is a confirmed corpus defect, although
  actual model reliance on it was not separately quantified.
- **Long-target supervision:** most supervised tokens teach a shared narrative;
  only the final few tokens encode the label. The model can reduce loss by learning
  fluent class-associated wording and then continue consistently into the answer.
  In C1, the supervised suffix directly teaches only class plus EOS.
- **Images still matter:** image swaps changed freely generated descriptions and
  classes, and radar context ablation reduced recall. Therefore the failure is not
  that the model ignores all pixels; it combines visual sensitivity with brittle
  text templates, shortcut cues and weak rejection support.

The missing causal experiment is item 3: same corpus and recipe, changing only the
target from full CoT to class-only. Until that is run, report the current conclusion
as a combined **data-quality + target-format** failure, not “CoT is always worse.”
