### Controlled target-format ablation

This was the causal study missing from the earlier C1-versus-R1 comparison. Both
new arms used the same 1,960 neutralized images, prompts, ordering, base model,
LoRA recipe, seed and two epochs; only the assistant target changed.

| Arm | Accuracy | Macro-F1 | Radar F1 | Missile F1 | Other F1 | Background F1 | Non-target acceptance |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Full CoT | .266 | .133 | .060 | .410 | .029 | .031 | .961 |
| Class-only | .590 | .517 | .682 | .710 | .617 | .061 | .273 |

Direct class-only supervision therefore wins strongly on this corpus. Full CoT
collapsed mostly to missile: missile recall was 1.000 but precision only .258.
However, background recall remained only .031 even for class-only, confirming that
the R1 rejection corpus itself is also weak. The result supports a combined
target-format and negative-data diagnosis, not a claim that every reasoning target
is harmful.
