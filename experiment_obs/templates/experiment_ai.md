## Setup and execution

failed attempts, observed/inferred/unknown causes and missing results.
Status must reflect observations: planned / running / completed / failed / unknown.

## Decision and next proposal
Interpretation, limitations, evidence links, supporting paths/hashes/sizes when known.
Record actual work date separately from folder date; no invented runs or metrics.
